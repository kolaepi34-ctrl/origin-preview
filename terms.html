Terms of Service

Last updated: 16 August 2026

These terms cover the use of this website and the quote requests sent through it. They are not the moving contract — that is signed separately before the crew loads.

Quotes

A price given on the phone or by email is an estimate based on what you tell us: the addresses, the date, the access and the inventory. Local moves are billed hourly, per crew, with a two-hour minimum and travel time stated up front. If the job turns out larger or the access harder than described, the time changes and so does the final amount.

What we move and what we decline

  • Upright pianos: yes, with a four-mover crew and a $200 heavy-item surcharge. Grand and baby grand pianos: no.
  • Safes: up to 250 lbs including stairs; 250–400 lbs first floor only; over 400 lbs declined.
  • Gym and shop equipment: up to 400 lbs, first floor only.
  • Pool tables: carried only when already disassembled.
  • We do not disconnect or reconnect appliances, medical equipment or IT hardware.
  • We move within Colorado only. No interstate moves.

Liability

In Colorado a household goods mover provides valuation, not insurance. The default level of liability is $0.60 per pound per article. Higher valuation, including full-value protection, must be arranged before the crew loads — ask on the quote call.

Cancellation

Plans change. Tell us as early as you can so the crew can be given another job; there is no charge for cancelling ahead of move day.
