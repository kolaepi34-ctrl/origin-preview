Common questions

Answers before you call

How is the price calculated?

By the hour, per crew, with a two-hour minimum. Travel time is included and stated up front. Heavy-item surcharge $200 where it applies.

Do you move grand pianos?

No. Grand and baby grand pianos need disassembly and a specialty rig. Upright pianos we move with a four-mover crew, dolly, blankets and straps.

What about safes and gym equipment?

Safes up to 250 lbs including stairs. Between 250 and 400 lbs — first floor only. Over 400 lbs we decline. Gym and shop equipment: up to 400 lbs, first floor only.

Do you move out of state?

No. We work inside Colorado only, and we say so on the first call rather than taking a deposit.

What is included with the truck?

Moving blankets, shrink wrap, dollies and floor runners. Furniture is disassembled and put back together at the new address.

How does liability work in Colorado?

A household goods mover provides valuation, not insurance. The default level is $0.60 per pound per article; a higher level has to be arranged before the crew loads.
