You ask, we answer

Common moving questions.

How much does a move cost?

Most local moves are billed at an hourly crew rate; long-distance is a flat quote. The final price depends on your inventory, distance, stairs and access. Tell us about your move and we’ll give you a transparent quote up front — no surprises.

What heavy items do you take?

Upright pianos with a four-mover crew and a $200 surcharge — grand pianos we decline. Safes up to 250 lbs including stairs, 250–400 lbs ground floor only, over 400 lbs we decline. Pool tables only if already taken apart. Gym and shop equipment, machinery and workshop tools up to 400 lbs, ground floor only. We also take antiques and artwork, large appliances, motorcycles and ATVs, and aquariums and plants once they are empty, drained and dry.

Do you do interstate moves?

No. We move within Colorado only — Denver and 50 miles around it, plus city-to-city moves inside the state. If you are leaving Colorado we will say so on the first call rather than waste your time.

Do you offer storage or junk removal?

No. We do not run a warehouse and we do not haul junk. We move your things from one address to another — that is what our crews and trucks are built for.

Do you pack for me?

Yes — full packing, partial packing, or unpacking at the new place with the rubbish taken away. We sell boxes, paper, tape and mattress bags, and crate mirrors and artwork on site.

Do you help if I rented my own truck?

Yes. Labour-only crews load your rental truck or POD, unload a container that has arrived, rearrange furniture inside the house, or carry up a walk-up with no lift. You supply the vehicle, we supply the muscle.
