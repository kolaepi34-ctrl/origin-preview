Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

This page explains what happens to the details you send us through this website. Origin Movers LLC, 911 S Zeno Way Unit 106, Aurora, CO 80017.

What we collect

  • What you type into a quote form: name, phone, email, move date, addresses and any notes.
  • Photos or video you choose to upload for an estimate.
  • Standard analytics: pages visited, device type, and how you reached the site.

Why we collect it

  • To answer your request and price the move.
  • To reach you by phone, text or email about that request.
  • To measure which pages and ads bring calls, so we do not waste money on the ones that do not.

Who sees it

Your request reaches our office by email. We do not sell your details and we do not pass them to other moving companies or lead brokers. Service providers that make the site work — the form delivery service, the hosting provider and the analytics provider — process the data on our behalf.

Calls, texts and email

By sending a request you agree that we may contact you about it by phone, text message or email. Message and data rates may apply. You can ask us to stop at any time — reply STOP to a text, or write to the address below.

Cookies

Analytics cookies are off until you accept them in the banner. Declining does not change how the site works.

How long we keep it

Quote requests are kept while we are in touch about the move and for our records afterwards. Ask us to delete yours and we will.

Your choices

Write to originmoversllc@gmail.com or call 720-462-0407 to see, correct or delete what we hold about you.
