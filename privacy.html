Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

This policy explains what Origin Movers LLC does with the information you give us through this website, by phone, by text or by email. It is written in plain language because a moving company should be as clear about your data as it is about its prices.

What we collect

  • Contact details you type into a quote or contact form: name, phone number, email, the address you are moving from and to, the move date and the service you need.
  • Move details: inventory lists, the size of the home or office, notes about access — stairs, elevators, parking — requests for special handling, and anything you send us by phone, email or text.
  • Payment details, when a move is invoiced. Card data is handled by our payment provider; full card numbers are not stored on our side.
  • Technical data collected automatically: IP address, browser and device type, the page that sent you here and the pages you opened. This comes from ordinary web logs, analytics and advertising tags.

Why we use it

  • To price your move and answer your questions.
  • To schedule the crew, run the job and invoice it.
  • To call or text you about a booking you asked for — see the SMS section below.
  • To handle a claim or a question after the move is finished.
  • To see which pages and which ads actually bring calls, and to fix the ones that do not.
  • To meet our legal, tax and transport-regulation obligations in Colorado.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not hand it to other companies for their own marketing. Lead brokers do not get your details from us.

  • The crew assigned to your move — only what they need to reach the address and move your things.
  • Service providers that keep the business running: hosting, the form delivery service, scheduling, phone and text services, email and payments. Each is bound by confidentiality terms.
  • Insurers or claim administrators, if a claim is filed on your shipment.
  • Authorities, when the law, a subpoena or a valid government request requires it, or where we must protect our rights, safety or property.

Keeping it safe

The site runs over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Access to requests is limited to the people who need it to do their job. No system is perfectly secure, so we do not claim absolute safety — we keep what we need for as long as the purposes above require, or as long as the law says.

Your choices

Ask us and we will show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Ask us to stop sending marketing messages and we will stop. Colorado residents have rights under the Colorado Privacy Act, including the right to know what categories of data we collect and to opt out of targeted advertising. Exercising any of these rights costs you nothing and changes nothing about the service you get. Some records we must keep — transport regulation and tax rules require it.

Write to originmoversllc@gmail.com or call 720-462-0407.

Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

Cookies

The site uses cookies to work properly, to remember your choices, to count visits and to measure our advertising, including Google Analytics and Google Ads tags. Analytics cookies stay off until you accept them in the banner. You can block or delete cookies in your browser — the site will still work, some measurement will not.

Text messages

When you give us your phone number and tick the consent box in a form, you agree that Origin Movers may text you about your estimate, your booking, crew arrival and other service updates at that number, including messages sent by an automated system.

  • Agreeing to texts is not a condition of buying anything from us.
  • How often we text depends on your move and your questions.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to stop, HELP for help.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Mobile numbers and SMS consent are never shared or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing. Numbers collected for texting are used by Origin Movers and by the providers that deliver our messages, and by nobody else.

Changes to this policy

We update this page when the way we work changes. The revised version appears here with a new "last updated" date.
